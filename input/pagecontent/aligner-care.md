# Aligner Planning and Progress

`AlignerCarePlanDE` remains a specialization of the existing KFO care-plan
root. Planned attachments identify one FDI tooth. Interproximal reduction
identifies both adjacent FDI teeth and carries the planned amount as a UCUM
Quantity in millimetres.

`AlignerProgressObservationDE` records the assessed stage as a positive integer
and the clinical tracking outcome as tracking as planned, tracking deviation,
or attachment loss. Manufacturer setup identifiers may be ordinary identifiers
or references; they do not become canonical terminology.

The profiles contain no GOZ triggers, coverage tiers, court-derived rules, or
automatic suitability decisions.
